# BTC/USD Video Strategy Evidence

## Video 1: Bitcoin Institutional Strategy (Step By Step)

The video prefers an 8-hour macro structure timeframe; 6H may be used, but 8H is preferred to reduce micro-swings and wicks. During rapid volume-driven movement, the analyst may lower the timeframe to 4H, 1H, or 30M until swings and ranges are visible. The strategy trades with the macro trend and uses supply/demand structures, waiting for a structure break and retracement.

Ranges are defined by a swing high and swing low. After a swing high breaks and price returns inside, the new high becomes the top of the range and the swing low that forced the move becomes the bottom. Fib levels are 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, and 1. Discount is the lower quarter, premium is the upper quarter. Extreme demand/supply should be located inside those quarters. Previous swing highs and lows are liquidity.

The strategy requires a clear break of structure and a deep retracement into discount or premium. Longs are sought in discount after retracement; shorts in premium. Stops go slightly beyond the swing that originated the extreme level. TP1 targets the prior range or a 1:1 level; TP2 targets movement outside the range and clearance of the prior swing liquidity. The video mentions moving to break-even after TP1 but cautions against moving too aggressively. A 1:1 first target is the minimum stated in this video.

Optional concepts include reversal after opposite structure break, and a 50% Fib trade when price does not reach the extreme quarter. No sessions/news rule was stated. Waiting is required when structure has not broken or consolidation immediately follows a reaction.

## Video 2: I EXPOSE my BEST Crypto Trading Strategy

The video uses 1W -> 1D -> 4H -> 1H -> 15M. Weekly is the most important macro direction. The analyst identifies the 1H trend, sets alerts at levels where the 1H trend would break, then drops to 15M after that alert triggers. Entry occurs when 15M structure breaks in the anticipated direction.

Stops are above the previous high for shorts or below the previous low for longs. The video aims for 1:2 or 1:3 and moves stop to break-even when price has traveled approximately 50% toward the target. Targets are set from the selected RR rather than a detailed opposing-zone hierarchy. The only explicit WAIT rule is to wait for the 1H trend break before looking for a lower-timeframe entry. No explicit zones, liquidity, sessions, or news rules are stated.

## Video 3: HOW TO MAKE 80K A YEAR TRADING BITCOIN BTCUSD STRATEGY

The video begins on a higher timeframe to identify dead zones and major key levels, then uses 4H to find areas touched multiple times, generally two or three important areas. It moves to 1H for patterns. Dead zones/order blocks/major key levels are areas where price repeatedly fails to break. Trendlines connect multiple touchpoints to form support/resistance patterns.

The strategy waits for a breakout and then a retest/pullback before entry. Triangle/wave patterns may use a five-leg/touch count as a possible correction-ending signal. No explicit liquidity, stop, target, or RR rule is stated. Crypto manipulation and fundamental news are acknowledged. The next move often occurs during London or New York after a corrective phase. The initial breakout must not be chased; the pullback/retest is required.
